# Online Shopping Process Improvement

## Project Overview
This project focuses on understanding why customers leave an online shopping website without completing their purchases.

## Business Problem
Many customers add products to their shopping cart but do not complete the checkout process. This may result in lost sales and a poor customer experience.

## Business Objective
The objective is to identify possible reasons for incomplete purchases and suggest improvements to make the checkout process easier and more user-friendly.

## Scope
- Understand the customer shopping journey.
- Identify possible checkout problems.
- Analyze reasons for cart abandonment.
- Suggest solutions to improve customer experience.

## Expected Outcome
A smoother checkout process, improved customer experience, and potentially more completed orders.
